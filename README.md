# ARCEDians Vault for Android

The password manager app for ARCED Foundation staff. It is the
[Bitwarden Android app](https://github.com/bitwarden/android), rebranded and preset to ARCED's
own server, `https://vault.arced.foundation` (Vaultwarden).

**Install:** open <https://vault.arced.foundation/get> on your phone (or scan its QR code),
download the APK and open it. Android asks once to allow installs from your browser. The app
checks for updates when it starts and offers the new APK.

- Android 10 or newer. No Google Play Services needed.
- iPhone: not yet.

## For developers

- [`ARCED.md`](ARCED.md): what is changed from upstream, signing, release steps and the monthly

- [`docs/ARCED-DEVELOPING.md`](docs/ARCED-DEVELOPING.md): setting up a PC, building, testing
